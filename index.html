AI Engineering AI Document Assistant

A Java-native RAG assistant that answers questions over documents such as invoices and contracts. A Spring Boot ingestion pipeline parses, chunks, and embeds documents into a PGVector store.

At query time, Spring AI's ChatClient with a QuestionAnswerAdvisor retrieves the most relevant chunks and calls Amazon Bedrock — Claude for chat, Titan for embeddings — to return source-grounded, cited answers rather than unsupported guesses.

Stack: Java, Spring Boot, Spring AI, Amazon Bedrock (Claude, Titan Embeddings), PGVector, PostgreSQL
